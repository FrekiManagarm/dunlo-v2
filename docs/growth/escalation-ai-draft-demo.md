# Escalation AI Draft Demo

Purpose: a 2-3 minute Loom focused only on the escalation workflow. Use the landing section mockup or the `/escalations` dashboard screen as the recording target.

## Recording Setup

- Open the escalation interface with the Pending list visible.
- Select Lucas Fontaine / Northstar Ledger, amount `€956`.
- Keep the AI draft panel, subject, body, and Send / Regenerate / Dismiss actions in frame.
- Record at 1440x900 or similar desktop size. Avoid showing browser bookmarks or local dev tooling.

## Script

### 0:00-0:20 - Context

"You have a customer paying around $800 a month. Their card just declined. Here is what happens in Dunlo."

### 0:20-0:45 - Pending Escalations

Show the Pending list with high-value failed payments. Click Lucas Fontaine / Northstar Ledger.

"This failed payment crossed my $500 escalation threshold, so Dunlo did not keep it in the normal automated sequence."

### 0:45-1:30 - AI Draft

Show the subject and body.

"Dunlo detected that this is a high-value customer and drafted a personal email automatically. It includes the customer name, the payment amount, a human tone, and my signature. The customer receives a note from me, but I did not have to start from a blank page."

### 1:30-2:00 - Regenerate

Click Regenerate and pause on the updated draft.

"If I do not like the tone, I can regenerate it in one click. The draft stays editable, so I can also tweak any line manually."

### 2:00-2:30 - Send

Click Send and show the status changing to Sent.

"When it looks good, I send it. That is the whole workflow: detect the important failure, draft the founder email, review it, send it."

### 2:30-3:00 - Close

"Your most valuable customer got a personal email from you in under 30 seconds, without you having to write it from scratch. Automated recovery for the small failures. Your personal touch, drafted by AI, for the ones that matter."
