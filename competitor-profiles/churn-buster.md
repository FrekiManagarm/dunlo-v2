## Positioning & Messaging

**Primary value proposition**: Advanced dunning and cancel flows guided by retention specialists.

**Target audience**: Larger subscription businesses that want a partner, not just a lightweight tool.

**Positioning angle**: Expert-led retention system with adaptive dunning, cancel flows, transparent attribution, and high-touch optimization.

**Key messaging themes**:
- Better than default platform retries because it adapts by decline code and segment.
- Strategic retention partner with 10+ years of data.
- High-touch support and ongoing optimization.

## Product & Features

- Dunning/passive churn recovery.
- Cancel flows/active churn recovery.
- Decline-code and segment-based recovery paths.
- Analytics and attribution.
- Concierge setup and account reviews.
- Integrations include Stripe, Shopify, Recharge, Loop, Skio, Smartrr, Subbly, and more.

## Strengths

- Mature category specialist with strong proof and recognizable logos.
- Broader passive + active churn story.
- Strong for brands with enough scale to justify high-touch retention work.
- Multi-platform coverage beyond Stripe.

## Weaknesses

- Can feel heavy for a solo founder or small Stripe SaaS.
- Dunning-only pricing is not self-serve/public.
- Positioning increasingly spans ecommerce and active churn, which leaves room for a focused Stripe SaaS message.

**Where they are strong vs. Dunlo**: Brand maturity, proof, data, expert support, broader retention suite.

**Where Dunlo can win**: Simpler Stripe-first setup, founder-oriented workflow, beta-free entry point, and clearer focus on failed-payment visibility for SaaS operators.
