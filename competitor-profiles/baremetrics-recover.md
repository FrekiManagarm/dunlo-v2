## Positioning & Messaging

**Primary value proposition**: Recover failed payments with campaigns, in-app reminders, payment forms, and analytics inside the subscription analytics dashboard.

**Target audience**: SaaS teams that want metrics and dunning in the same tool.

**Positioning angle**: Analytics-first recovery add-on.

**Key messaging themes**:
- Failed payments put around 9% of MRR at risk.
- Recover can be enabled from the Baremetrics dashboard.
- Payment processors send too few or too generic notifications.

## Product & Features

- Customizable email and SMS campaigns.
- In-app reminders and paywalls.
- Credit-card capture forms.
- One-click recovery links.
- Recovery analytics.
- Integrations include Stripe, Braintree, Chargebee, Recurly, Shopify, app stores, QuickBooks Online, Xero, and Universal Connector.

## Strengths

- Strong if the buyer already wants subscription analytics.
- Recovery lives near MRR/churn reporting.
- Public pricing is relatively understandable.
- Broad payment integration coverage.

## Weaknesses

- Payment recovery is an add-on to an analytics suite, not a focused standalone product.
- Minimum effective cost is higher than the add-on alone if the team does not already use Baremetrics.
- Less sharp for teams that only want a Stripe recovery workflow.

## Competitive Implications for Dunlo

**Where they are strong vs. Dunlo**: Analytics suite, established brand, broader metrics context.

**Where Dunlo can win**: Dedicated recovery workflow, less analytics-suite overhead, Stripe-specific copy and escalation, beta-free wedge.

**Opportunity**: "Baremetrics Recover alternative" should target founders who already have analytics elsewhere and only need failed-payment recovery.
