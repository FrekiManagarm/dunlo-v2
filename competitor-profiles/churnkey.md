## Positioning & Messaging

**Primary value proposition**: A modern retention suite combining cancel flows, payment recovery, reactivation, customer health, and metrics.

**Target audience**: SaaS/subscription teams that want one tool for churn reduction, not just failed-payment dunning.

**Positioning angle**: AI/ML retention automation with precision retries and omnichannel campaigns.

**Key messaging themes**:
- Recover up to 89% of failed payments.
- Fixed fees, never a take rate.
- ML-optimized retries.
- Broader churn suite with cancel flows and reactivation.

## Product & Features

## Strengths

- Strong feature breadth across voluntary and involuntary churn.
- Modern product/category positioning.
- Payment recovery is not just emails; it includes retries, SMS, hosted flows, metrics, and segmentation.
- Strong for teams ready to centralize retention.

## Weaknesses

- More expensive and broader than a founder may need for Stripe-only failed payments.
- The broad retention story can feel like overkill for small SaaS teams.
- Annual-billed starter pricing may create more friction than Dunlo's beta-free motion.

## Competitive Implications for Dunlo

**Where they are strong vs. Dunlo**: Breadth, AI/ML story, cancel flows, mature retention suite.

**Where Dunlo can win**: Narrow Stripe-specific job, simpler setup, lower commitment, founder-friendly workflow.

**Opportunity**: "Churnkey alternative for Stripe payment recovery" should be honest: choose Churnkey for all-in-one retention; choose Dunlo when failed Stripe payments are the immediate leak.
