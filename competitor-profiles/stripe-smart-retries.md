## Positioning

**Primary value proposition**: Stripe Smart Retries uses AI to choose better retry times for failed invoices and reduce involuntary churn without manual intervention.

**Target audience**: SaaS and subscription teams already using Stripe Billing.

**Positioning angle**: Native retry infrastructure, not a separate customer-recovery product.

## Product & Features

- AI retry timing for failed invoices.
- Custom retry schedules.
- Recovery analytics and customer emails inside Stripe's broader revenue recovery suite.
- Webhook events such as `invoice.payment_failed`.
- Automations for custom dunning and team notifications.

## Pricing

Stripe Smart Retries is not sold as a standalone competitor page product. It is part of Stripe Billing revenue recovery controls.

## Strengths

- Native to Stripe, so setup friction is low for Stripe Billing users.
- No new vendor required.
- Strong for silent recovery of soft declines.
- Good default for early teams with low failure volume.

## Weaknesses

- Does not solve the whole customer-facing workflow by itself.
- Hard declines and missing payment methods still need customer action.
- Limited positioning around founder escalation, account review, and nuanced customer copy.
- Can make the problem feel "handled" while failed accounts still need follow-up.

## Competitive Implications for Dunlo

**Where Stripe is strong vs. Dunlo**: Native, trusted, no extra tool.

**Where Dunlo can win**: Dunlo should position as the layer after Smart Retries: failure-aware emails, founder review, recovered revenue visibility, and a clear queue of accounts at risk.

**Best page angle**: Keep the existing Stripe Smart Retries alternative page narrow and honest: "Do not replace Stripe. Add the recovery layer Stripe does not cover."
