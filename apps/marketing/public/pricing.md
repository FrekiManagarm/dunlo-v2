# Pricing - Dunlo

Dunlo is free during beta. Published prices show the intended pricing direction after beta.

## Solo

- Price: $19/month
- Best for: founders validating recovery
- MRR fit: less than $5k MRR
- AI escalations: 5 per month
- Team members: 1
- Features:
  - All failure-code sequences
  - Unlimited sequence steps
  - Stripe failure-code detection
  - Recovery emails by failure type
  - Secure payment update links
  - No recovered-revenue percentage

## Starter
